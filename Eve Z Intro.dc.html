what i do

Trust before automation.

I build systems that turn messy, high-volume operational data into decisions people can actually trust — combining structured data quality work, applied AI, and urban/transportation domain expertise.

Data quality & QC pipelines

Detecting anomalies, validating reliability, and structuring manual review into repeatable workflows, whether the data source is traffic detectors, government open-data portals, or public spending records.

Agentic AI & trusted reporting

Designing AI systems where verified data comes first and the model's role is narrowing, not inventing — grounded summaries, constrained context, and human review built in by design rather than bolted on after.

Urban & transportation analytics

Applying geospatial and statistical methods (GIS, regression, land-use modeling) to real planning and policy questions, from signal timing to transit-oriented development to green infrastructure siting.

I've applied this across production engineering work (VDOT signal operations), published research (Remote Sensing), and self-directed tooling (agentic BI for public transportation data) — the throughline is always: earn the trust before you automate the judgment.
